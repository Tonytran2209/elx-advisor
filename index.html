The Hydrogen® Bottleneck

Green hydrogen exists. It's just too expensive to matter.

Electrolyzers split water into hydrogen using electricity. But they need DC power — and the grid produces AC. The rectifier required to convert it is expensive. Even when wind energy is practically free, the electrolyzer can't take advantage of it.
